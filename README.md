## 🌍 What is Lumi?

Meeting new people shouldn't be complicated.

With Lumi, you create a profile, choose what kind of connection you're looking for, and discover people who may be a good match.

Whether you want to **make friends, chat, flirt, or find something serious**, Lumi gives you a simple way to meet people through Telegram.

---

## ✨ How it works

**👤 Create your profile**  
Tell Lumi about yourself and what you're looking for.

**🔎 Discover people**  
Explore profiles based on your preferences and discovery settings.

**❤️ Like or pass**  
Like someone you're interested in or move on to the next profile.

**💫 Match**  
When the interest is mutual, Lumi creates a match.

**💬 Connect**  
Take the next step and get to know your match on Telegram.
